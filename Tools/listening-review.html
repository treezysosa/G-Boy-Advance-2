G Boy Advance 2 · Preset listening review

Original synthesis, rendered from your current bank. All sounds start unreviewed. Measurements flag listening candidates; they do not judge musical quality.

How to use this review

Select and play a sound, then compare it with its closest numerical neighbor. Rate the sound and write what should change. A/B level matching only turns the louder clip down. The chord retains the preset's own mono/poly mode. Soft and strong middle notes use the same pitch. Very long release/effects tails may exceed the 20-second excerpt.

Factory presets remain unchanged. The gain figure is a bounded proposal based on sound-family LUFS, not an applied correction. Save feedback with Export before moving files or switching browsers; browser storage is a convenience rather than a backup. Space plays/pauses; J/K choose previous/next outside text fields. Take listening breaks.
